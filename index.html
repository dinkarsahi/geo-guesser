SpotOn

A geography guessing game. You are shown a place — a city name, a flag, a currency, a company logo, a population figure, a tube station or a live clock — and you spin a 3D globe and click where you think it belongs. Every game is five rounds, and each round is marked out of 100 on how close you were.

Seven games

  • City Spotter — with just the name, spot where the city is.
  • Flag Spotter — spot which country the flag belongs to.
  • Currency Spotter — spot a country that spends it.
  • Corporate HQ Spotter — spot where a company is headquartered.
  • Population Spotter — spot the country from its population.
  • Tube Station Spotter — find the station on the London Underground.
  • Time Zone Spotter — read the clock, spot a country keeping it.

Three ways to play

  • Today's Round — one game a day, the same one for everybody, with a leaderboard.
  • Duel a Friend — a room, a code, and the same five rounds at the same moment.
  • All Games — any of the seven, at your own pace, scored nowhere.
